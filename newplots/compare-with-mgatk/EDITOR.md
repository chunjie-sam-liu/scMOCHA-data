Dear Dr. Pei,

Thank you for submitting your paper to Cell Metabolism. I have read the manuscript with interest, discussed it with the editorial team and consulted with a scientific advisor (in confidence), who echoed our concerns. Whilst they appreciated that you've generated a resource of mtDNA variants across human hematopoietic tissues, they noted concerns regarding the reliance on mgatk, which is biased toward detecting higher-heteroplasmy mutations that are more likely to be subject to functional selection, and missing many mutations that would be tolerated at lower heteroplasmy.​ Based on this consideration, we concluded that your manuscript is a better fit for another journal.  ​

Lastly, we do appreciate your interest in publishing with Cell Metabolism. I hope we will have an opportunity to work with you again and that you will consider submitting future work to us.
